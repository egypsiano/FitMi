Bench Press

Chest Shoulders Triceps Compound Barbell

Description

The bench press is a weight training exercise that involves lying on a bench and pushing a weight upwards with your arms. It primarily works the pectoralis major muscle as well as the anterior deltoids, and triceps.

Primary Benefits

  • Builds upper body strength
  • Increases chest muscle mass
  • Improves pushing power
  • Enhances shoulder stability

Proper Form

  1. Lie on bench with eyes under the bar
  2. Grip slightly wider than shoulder width
  3. Retract shoulder blades and arch slightly
  4. Lower bar to mid-chest with control
  5. Press bar back up in a slight arch

Common Mistakes

  • Bouncing the bar off the chest
  • Lifting hips off the bench
  • Flaring elbows excessively
  • Not using a full range of motion
