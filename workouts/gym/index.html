Gym Workout Routines

Explore various gym workout routines tailored for different goals. Remember to warm up before each session and cool down afterwards. Consult with a professional if you are new to exercise or have any health concerns.

This routine targets all major muscle groups. Perform 3 sets of 8-12 repetitions for each exercise, unless otherwise noted. Rest 60-90 seconds between sets.

1. Squats

Description: A compound exercise that primarily trains the thighs, hips and buttocks, quadriceps femoris muscle and hamstrings.

Upper Body Focus

This routine emphasizes developing strength and muscle in the upper body. Perform 3 sets of 10-15 repetitions. Rest 60 seconds between sets.

1. Overhead Press

Description: A weight training exercise, typically performed while standing, in which a weight is pressed straight upwards from racking position until the arms are locked out overhead.
