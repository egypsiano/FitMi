Home Workout Routines

Effective workout routines you can do from the comfort of your home. No equipment needed for many of these! Always ensure you have enough space and a safe environment. Consult with a professional if you are new to exercise or have any health concerns.

Bodyweight Basics

A foundational routine using only your bodyweight. Focus on form. Perform 3 sets of 10-15 repetitions for each exercise. Rest 45-60 seconds between sets.

1. Push-ups

Description: An exercise to develop upper body strength, primarily working the pectoral muscles, triceps, and anterior deltoids. Can be modified (e.g., on knees).

Quick Cardio Blast (15 Minutes)

Get your heart rate up with this short and effective cardio routine. Perform each exercise for 45 seconds, followed by 15 seconds of rest. Repeat the circuit 2-3 times.

1. Jumping Jacks

Description: A full-body exercise that can be done almost anywhere. It's a great way to warm up or get your heart rate up quickly.

3. Burpees (Modified if needed)

Description: A full-body exercise used in strength training and as an aerobic exercise. It is performed in four steps: begin in a standing position, drop into a squat position with your hands on the ground, kick your feet back into a plank position, immediately return your feet to the squat position, and stand up from the squat position.
